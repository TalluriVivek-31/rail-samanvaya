# RAIL SAMNVAY (रेल समन्वय)
## AI-Powered Automatic Block Planning for Fixed Railway Infrastructure
### Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Problem Statement 26027

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Principle

**Rail Samnvay** is an intelligent, G&SR-compliant decision-support system designed to solve the critical railway dilemma: **maximizing infrastructure maintenance time while minimizing passenger & freight train detention**.

### The Core Operational Principle

> **USER REQUESTS MAINTENANCE WORK → SYSTEM UNDERSTANDS RAILWAY CONTEXT → TECHNICAL VERIFICATION & PLANNING OPTIMIZATION → RECOMMENDED BLOCK WINDOW → AUTHORIZED OPERATING / CONTROL AUTHORITY AUTHORIZES & SCHEDULES THE BLOCK**

A field engineer submits a **Maintenance Requirement**, **NEVER an automatically created or scheduled Block**. The system evaluates engineering dependencies, spatial overlaps, live train movements, and safety clearances before an authorized Railway Officer grants or schedules any track possession.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Railway Operational Authority Notice:**  
> **"Rail Samnvay models an Authorized Operating / Control Authority for operational validation and block authorization. The exact competent authority and workflow can vary by block type, division and applicable railway operating rules."**

## 2. Block Authority & Role Distinction Matrix

| Role | Prototype Designation & Name | Operational Authority & Responsibilities | UI Actions Allowed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Field Engineer** | `P.Way`, `S&T`, `TRD` Engineers (e.g. A. K. Sharma) | Maintenance requesters. Identifies physical asset defects, specifies chainage, track machines, power isolation needs. **Cannot approve or schedule blocks**. | `Create Maintenance Requirement`, `Resubmit Corrected Requisition` |
| **Planning Officer** | `Planning Officer` (M. K. Rao, DOM Planning) | Reviews requisitions, verifies isolations, executes AI prioritization, conflict analysis, CPM, and CP-SAT optimizer. Generates **Recommended Block Window** and submits to Control. **Does NOT approve or grant blocks**. | `RUN PRIORITIZATION`, `RUN CONFLICT ANALYSIS`, `RUN OPTIMIZATION`, `GENERATE RECOMMENDATION & SEND TO CONTROL`, `Request Revision` |
| **Operating Control** | `COA / Operations` (P. Murthy, Chief Train Controller) | **Authorized Operating / Control Authority**. Evaluates corridor headway impact, reviews train conflict graphs, validates operating feasibility, **Authorizes & Schedules Block**, issues official Block Memos, grants possessions (`Block Started`), and accepts line clearance (`Block Released`). | `REVIEW OPERATIONAL IMPACT`, `VIEW TRAIN CONFLICTS`, `AUTHORIZE & SCHEDULE BLOCK`, `GRANT POSSESSION (BLOCK STARTED)`, `RELEASE POSSESSION` |
| **System Admin** | `MASTER` (V. Ramanathan, PCOM) | **System Administrator / Full-Access Prototype Role**. Superuser access across all screens for evaluation. If MASTER bypasses operating control to authorize/schedule a block, the system explicitly logs **`[Administrative Override]`** in the audit ledger. | `ADMINISTRATIVE OVERRIDE: AUTHORIZE BLOCK`, `DELETE BLOCK`, full system settings |

## 3. End-to-End Operational Pipeline

```text
       [ Field Maintenance Engineer ]
                     │
                     ▼
       1. Maintenance Requisition Form
          (P.Way / TRD / S&T / Mechanical)
                     │
                     ▼
       2. Location Intelligence Engine
          (Chainage normalization, Station Yard Limits, Affected Tracks)
                     │
                     ▼
       3. AI-Assisted Priority Scoring
          (Criticality 35%, Urgency 25%, Derailment Risk 20%, Traffic 10%, Resources 10%)
                     │
                     ▼
       4. Department Technical Verification
          (Isolation verification, Equipment readiness, Multi-dept joint review)
                     │
                     ▼
       5. Dynamic Timetable & RailRadar Train Movement Analysis
          (Scheduled passenger paths, Goods freight forecasts, Live GPS delays)
                     │
                     ▼
       6. 3-Way Spatial & Headway Conflict Detection
          (max(startA, startB) < min(endA, endB), 15-min safety margins)
                     │
                     ▼
       7. Critical Path Method (CPM) Activity Network
          (Power isolation, Signal disconnection, Parallel execution, Line clearance)
                     │
                     ▼
       8. Constrained Block Allocation Optimizer (CP-SAT)
          (Multi-department shadow bundling, Continuous closure limits <= 240m)
                     │
                     ▼
       9. Recommended Block Window Generated by Planning Officer
          ("Block Window Allocated" / Submitted to Operating Control via sendToControl)
                     │
                     ▼
      10. Authorized Operating / Control Authority Validation (COA / Operations)
          (Operational validation, Train path concurrence, Issues Block Memo: "Scheduled")
          * MASTER actions log [Administrative Override]
                     │
                     ▼
      11. Real-Time Execution Lifecycle
          (7-Stage G&SR Checklist: Block Started → WIP → Work Completed → Released)
                     │
                     ▼
      12. Permanent Non-Repudiable Audit Trail
          (Actor ID, Role, Timestamp, Prior State, New State, Reason)
```

## 5. Live Judge Demonstration Workflow (3-Minute Script)

1. **Step 1: Maintenance Requirement Creation (`P.Way Engineer` - A. K. Sharma)**:
   - Open **Requirements** (`#requests`) and click **+ New Maintenance Requirement**.
   - Input chainage `12/400` to `13/100` on `UP Main`.
   - Point out that **Station Identification** automatically detects `Mangalagiri (MAG) - Station Yard Limit Intersection`.
   - Submit the requisition. Observe status is **"Submitted"** (Maintenance Requirement, NOT an approved block).
   - Point out that creator **cannot self-approve** and field engineers **cannot schedule blocks**.
2. **Step 2: Technical Verification & Spatial Overlaps**:
   - Point out the **Priority Score (87/100)** calculated using the 5-factor deterministic formula.
   - Point out the **Multi-Department Spatial Overlap** warning: TRD has adjacent OHE maintenance on the same track.
3. **Step 3: Planning Review & Optimization (`Planning Officer` - M. K. Rao)**:
   - Switch role to **Planning Officer**.
   - Open **Approval Queue** (`#approval`). Notice button is **"GENERATE RECOMMENDATION & SEND TO CONTROL"** (no "APPROVE BLOCK").
   - Open **Planning Engine** (`#planning`).
   - Run CP-SAT Corridor Optimization: evaluates candidate windows against timetable trains and live RailRadar feeds.
   - Inspect the **CPM Activity Network**: shows Power Isolation (15m) $\rightarrow$ Parallel Track & OHE Work (120m) $\rightarrow$ Track Clearance (15m) $\rightarrow$ Line Release (5m). Total Float for TRD = 60m.
   - Click **SEND TO OPERATING CONTROL**. Status transitions to **"Block Window Allocated"** (Recommended Window).
4. **Step 4: Operational Validation & Block Authorization (`COA / Operations` - P. Murthy)**:
   - Switch role to **COA / Operations** (Chief Train Controller, Authorized Operating / Control Authority).
   - In Approval Queue or Planning Engine, review operational impact and headway conflict graphs.
   - Click **AUTHORIZE & SCHEDULE BLOCK**.
   - System assigns official Block Memo (e.g. `MEMO-BZA-8421`) and transitions status to **"Scheduled"**.
5. **Step 5: Prototype Superuser Demonstration (`MASTER` - V. Ramanathan)**:
   - Explain to judges that `MASTER` represents the System Administrator / Full-Access Prototype Superuser.
   - If MASTER directly schedules or overrides a block, show that the audit log registers **`[Administrative Override]`**.
6. **Step 6: Track Execution & Release in Control Room**:
   - Open **Execution** (`#execution`).
   - Progress through the 7 G&SR stages: `Block Started` $\rightarrow$ `Work in Progress` $\rightarrow$ `Work Completed` $\rightarrow$ `Block Released` $\rightarrow$ `Closed`.
7. **Step 7: Inspect Immutable Audit Trail & Cloud Sync**:
   - Open **Audit Trail** (`#audit`). Show the complete immutable log of all actions with actor stamps.
   - Point to the header **`CLOUD SYNC: ACTIVE`** pill demonstrating live Firebase Realtime Database persistence across all client sessions.
